## Small tools for everyday work

I make a Mac app and AI skills to help with coding, design, writing and research. Pick something useful and give it a try.

| Project | What you can do | Get started |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Codex Meter](https://github.com/xyloshin/codex-usage-meter)** | See your Codex allowance and reset time in the Mac menu bar, with usage history and low-balance alerts. | [Download for Mac](https://github.com/xyloshin/codex-usage-meter/releases/latest) |
| **[3VM](https://github.com/xyloshin/3vm)** | Turn a screenshot or idea into three design mockups, then choose a direction before building. | [Try the skill](https://github.com/xyloshin/3vm#install-in-codex) |
| **[Phrase](https://github.com/xyloshin/phrase)** | Work out what to say in an email, message or difficult conversation, with your goal and relationship in mind. | [Try the skill](https://github.com/xyloshin/phrase#get-started) |
| **[Similar](https://github.com/xyloshin/similar)** | Take a useful discovery and find where else the idea could help, with practical next steps. | [Try the skill](https://github.com/xyloshin/similar#try-it) |

Codex Meter requires macOS 14+ and a compatible Codex client signed in with ChatGPT. The skills run in your AI assistant; 3VM also needs image generation. All four projects are in beta. Setup details and limitations are in each repository.

Tried one? Open an issue in its repository with what helped or what got in the way. Please leave out private information.

Independent projects, not affiliated with OpenAI.
